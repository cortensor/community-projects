# Samsung Galaxy A-series — Not Charging: Field Notes

Original troubleshooting notes for the common "plugged in but not charging"
complaint on Samsung Galaxy A-series phones. Typical bench time is 20 to 40
minutes when the fault is in the charging path.

## Common causes

- A worn or damaged USB cable is the most frequent cause of intermittent charging.
- Lint packed into the charging port prevents the plug from seating fully.
- A faulty charging board (sub-board) breaks the charging path between the port and the main board.
- A swollen or dead battery cannot accept charge even when the charger works.
- Corroded connector contacts after liquid exposure interrupt current flow.

## Diagnostic procedure

1. Try a known-good charger and cable first; swap one part at a time.
2. Inspect the charging port under magnification and remove lint with a wooden toothpick.
3. Check for a charging icon and measure current draw with a USB meter.
4. Force restart the phone with Volume Down plus Power for 10 seconds.
5. If current stays at zero, open the device and reseat the charging board connector.
6. Measure battery voltage with a multimeter; below 3.4 V the battery is deeply discharged or dead.
7. Replace the charging board if the port is loose or the board shows corrosion.
8. Replace the battery if it is swollen or voltage does not recover on a bench supply.

## Tools

- USB current meter
- Wooden toothpick or plastic pick
- Screwdriver set (PH00)
- Tweezers
- Spudger and opening picks
- Multimeter
- Replacement charging board
- Replacement battery

## Warnings

- Warning: never charge a swollen battery; remove and recycle it safely.
- Do not use metal tools inside the charging port; they can short the pins.
- Disconnect the battery before removing the charging board.
- Opening the device may void the manufacturer warranty.
