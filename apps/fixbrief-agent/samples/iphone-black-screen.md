# iPhone — Black Screen, Phone Still On: Field Notes

Original troubleshooting notes for iPhones that vibrate or ring but show a
black screen. Bench time is 15 to 30 minutes for software and connector
causes; a full screen replacement takes about 45 minutes.

## Common causes

- A crashed display driver after an update can leave the screen black while the phone stays on.
- A loose display connector after a drop interrupts the image signal.
- A damaged screen assembly shows no image even though touch may still respond.
- A dead battery can look like a black screen when the phone fails to wake.

## Diagnostic procedure

1. Force restart with Volume Up, Volume Down, then hold the Side button until the logo appears.
2. Connect a charger for 15 minutes and check for vibration or sound.
3. Shine a flashlight at the screen; a faint image means the backlight path failed.
4. If the phone is detected by a computer, back up data before any repair.
5. Open the device, disconnect the battery, and reseat the display connectors.
6. Test with a known-good screen before ordering a replacement screen.
7. Replace the screen assembly if the test screen works and the original stays black.

## Tools

- Pentalobe screwdriver
- Suction cup
- Spudger and opening picks
- Tweezers
- Heat gun for adhesive
- Replacement screen

## Warnings

- Warning: disconnect the battery first; a live board can short during connector work.
- Do not pry near the battery; puncturing it can cause fire.
- Keep screws organized; a wrong-length screw can damage the board.
- Face ID components are paired to the board; handle the front sensor cable with care.
